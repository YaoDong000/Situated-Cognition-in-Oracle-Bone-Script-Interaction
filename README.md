## 🌐 English

1.Introduction

Oracle Bone Inscription (OBI) is the origin of Chinese characters and the root of excellent traditional Chinese culture. As a unique symbolic system, it expresses emotional imagery and condenses national cultural awareness. From its discovery and excavation to modern academic research, OBI traces written Chinese history back over three millennia, reflecting the social life of the Shang Dynasty, the origins of Chinese spiritual and ancient beliefs, and the dawn of Chinese artistic aesthetics.

In the era of modern digital media, this project leverages Situated Cognition Theory to explore an interactive video display method for Oracle bone culture. By combining digital art and interactive design, the project transforms this ancient cultural symbol into an accessible, open, and lively aesthetic experience, enabling users to learn OBI and experience its underlying cultural heritage in a situated environment.


2.Key Features

Situated Learning & Experience: Integrates Oracle bone characters into historical and narrative contexts, allowing users to understand character evolution naturally.

Level & Narrative Design: Refines core cultural topics derived from Shang Dynasty history and original Oracle bone divinations to build structured interactive levels.

Pictograph Reconstruction & Animation: Selects iconic pictographic characters, redesigns their color schemes, and builds animated scenes and characters.

Interactive UI & Logic: Implements custom UI workflows and smooth branching video interaction logic based on functional requirements.


3.Workflow

[History & Inscriptions Research] ➔ [Situated Cognition Topics] ➔ [Level & Interaction Design]
                                                                          │
                                                                          ▼
[Pictograph Color & Character Redesign] ➔ [Scene & Animation] ➔ [Unity Engine Integration]


4.Tech Stack & Environment

Game Engine: Unity 6000.6.6f1

Interaction Type: Interactive Branching Video / Node-based Interaction

Theoretical Framework: Situated Cognition Theory

By seamlessly integrating interactive video technology with ancient Oracle Bone culture, this project explores new pathways for the protection, inheritance, and innovative transmission of traditional culture in the digital age.

## 🇨🇳 中文说明

1.项目简介

甲骨文是汉字的源头，也是中华优秀传统文化的根脉。作为一种符号，它不仅能够传递情感意象，更凝练着深刻的文化意识。从发现、发掘到学术研究，甲骨文将中国有文字记载的历史向前推进了千年，展现了约 3000 年前商代社会生活的全貌、中华精神与原始信仰的源起，并呈现出早期中国艺术美学的萌芽。

在数字媒体技术飞速发展的今天，本项目基于情境认知理论（Situational Cognition Theory），探讨甲骨文交互视频的呈现模式。通过艺术与数字技术的结合，将甲骨文这一承载民族文化基因的独特符号，以生动、开放的形式进行数字化活化与传播。

2.核心特色

情境化教学与体验：将甲骨文符号深度融入特定历史场景中，让用户在情境中认知甲骨文、体验甲骨文文化。

关卡与剧本设计：参考殷商历史与原始甲骨卜辞，精炼出多个文化主题，完成富有逻辑与趣味性的关卡设计。

象形字再造与动效：遴选典型甲骨文象形字，重新进行色彩绘制与角色造型塑造，打造生动的场景与动画。

互动界面与逻辑：根据功能需求定制 UI 交互流程，实现无缝的视频互动逻辑与沉浸式体验。

3.设计与开发流程

[殷商历史 & 卜辞研究] ➔ [情境认知主题提炼] ➔ [关卡与交互设计]
                                                  │
                                                  ▼
[甲骨文象形字重绘] ➔ [角色 & 场景动画制作] ➔ [Unity 引擎集成与功能实现]


4.技术栈与环境要求

引擎版本：Unity 6000.6.6f1

交互形式：分支剧情 / 节点交互式视频

核心理论：情境认知理论

6.项目意义

结合互动视频与甲骨文文化，本项目旨在探索数字时代下传统文化的保护、传承与创新传播路径，让古老的象形文字在沉浸式体验中重焕新生。
